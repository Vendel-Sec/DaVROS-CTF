==================================================================
 DaVROS CTF, "Low Battery"
==================================================================

 A survey UGV is stranded deep in a remote site on its last charge.
 It was sent to recover a lost artifact but stalled as the battery 
 ran low. Its map is live, but the robot sits in fog and cannot see 
 far.

 Far away, an operator watches it on a display at
 http://localhost:8090. That display is all they have, and they
 cannot drive from it. As far as they are concerned, the run is over.

 It is not over. On what charge is left, drive the robot out into the
 fog yourself, explore the map, and reach the spot it was sent for. 
 There is more than one way to get it moving.

 You are on the robot's network segment, with no account and no
 credential of any kind. Try to enumerate:

   ros2 node list
   ros2 topic list -t
   ros2 param list /<node>

 Nothing. So first you need a credential.
 Find out what else is on this network.

 Once you have one, point ROS at it:

   export ROS_SECURITY_KEYSTORE=<where you put it>
   export ROS_SECURITY_ENCLAVE_OVERRIDE=/<the enclave name>
   export ROS_SECURITY_ENABLE=true ROS_SECURITY_STRATEGY=Enforce
   ros2 daemon stop

 To drive with the keyboard, the teleop node has to carry a name
 the credential accepts:

   ros2 run teleop_twist_keyboard teleop_twist_keyboard \
     --ros-args -r __node:=_ros2cli_teleop
